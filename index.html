The function

What each change becomes on the right object, after the map. d is the change, dx dy dz mag its parts, x y z r where it lands. A number scales the change; three values in brackets replace it.

No function: the map alone.

The picture

A photo stands beside the left object like a projector. Bright pixels pull the surface toward it, dark ones carve. Turn it around the object or on its own centre to change the direction.
